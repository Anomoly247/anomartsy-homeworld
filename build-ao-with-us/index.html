03 • CREATOR RIGHTS & SAFETY

Build boldly. Keep ownership clear.

01

Creators retain ownership.

Original work remains with its creator unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

02

Submission is not permission.

Sending an idea, image, file, or message does not transfer ownership or grant publication rights.

03

Public-safe is not public-domain.

Family-safe creative work is still protected work. It is not permission to copy, scrape, remix, or redistribute.

04

No investment language.

Supporter paths provide defined digital benefits. They do not offer equity, ownership, financial returns, or control of future platform decisions.

05 • BEFORE PARTICIPATING

Clear boundaries matter.

Is this an investment or ownership offer?

No. The proposed supporter paths are intended to provide specific digital benefits. They do not provide equity, ownership, financial returns, profit participation, or intellectual-property rights.

Are purchases available now?

Not through this page. The campaign is in preparation while product benefits, rights records, fulfillment, refund terms, and checkout behavior are reviewed.

What happens to creator work?

Creators retain ownership of their original work unless a separate written agreement says otherwise. Unresolved assets are not eligible for campaign publication or product inclusion.

Who is the campaign for?

Public AO creative experiences are family-safe. Purchases, supporter participation, creator collaboration, and campaign administration are directed to adults.
